Our Mission

Meta-analysis is the gold standard for evidence synthesis, but learning it traditionally requires expensive courses, university access, or expert mentors. We believe everyone deserves access to this knowledge.

By creating open-source skills that any AI agent can use, we're making advanced statistical training available to anyone with an internet connection— from medical students in rural clinics to researchers at major universities.
